Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Oh That's Why runs a small private tool (the "video-factory" app) that uploads our own videos to our own YouTube channels, Instagram accounts and Facebook Pages, and reads the view counts of those videos. This page explains what that tool does with data.

Who uses the app

Only the owner of the Oh That's Why channels. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else can sign in to it.

What data the app accesses

  • Upload access to our own YouTube channels (YouTube API Services), so finished videos can be posted.
  • Read access to our own channels' video statistics (views, likes, comments counts), so we can see which topics work.
  • Publishing and insights access to our own Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts, for the same two reasons.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store or sell any personal data of viewers.
  • We do not read, store or share anyone else's YouTube, Facebook or Instagram data.
  • We do not use cookies or tracking on this website.

How data is stored

Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in a private GitHub repository and are used only by our own automated jobs. Video statistics are stored as simple numbers in that same private repository. You can ask us to delete anything, and access can be revoked at any time from your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By watching our videos on YouTube you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

How to delete your data

The app stores no data about viewers or other users. If you have connected your own account to it and want everything removed:

  • Facebook and Instagram: open Facebook → Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations (or Apps and websites), find video-factory and click Remove.
  • Google and YouTube: open myaccount.google.com/permissions, find video-factory and click Remove access.
  • Or message us on Instagram and we delete any stored statistics within 7 days.
